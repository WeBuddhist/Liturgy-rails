Mañjughoṣa, lord of stainless wisdom,
Guhyapati, who subdues the entire host of Māras,
Tsongkhapa, crown jewel of the scholars of the Land of Snows,
At the feet of Lobsang Drakpa, I offer my prayer. ^1
