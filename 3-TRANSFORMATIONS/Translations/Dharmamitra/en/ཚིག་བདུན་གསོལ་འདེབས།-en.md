Hūṃ!
In the northwest of the land of Oḍḍiyāna,
Upon the stem and filaments of a lotus,
You attained the supreme, wondrous siddhi,
Renowned as the Lotus-Born.
Surrounded by a vast retinue of ḍākinīs,
I shall practice, following in your footsteps;
Please come in order to bestow your blessings.
Guru Padma Siddhi Hūṃ ^1
