# The Abridged Chronicle ^0

> ༄༅། །བཀའ་ཐང་བསྡུས་པ།

> ཨྱོན་སྐད་དུ༔ བུདྡྷ་དྷརྨ་སཾ་གྷ་ཡ༔
> བོད་སྐད་དུ༔སངས་རྒྱས་ཆོས་དང་དགེ་འདུན་ལ༔

In the language of Oḍḍiyāna: Buddha Dharma Saṅghāya
In the Tibetan language: To the Buddha, the Dharma, and the Saṅgha, ^1

> རྟག་ཏུ་གུས་པས་ཕྱག་འཚལ་ལོ༔
> འདྲེན་པའི་སྐྱེས་བུ་པདྨ་འབྱུང་གནས་ཀྱི༔
> གདུལ་བྱའི་དོན་དུ་འཁྲུང་རབས་འདི་བསྟན་པས༔
> སྐལ་ལྡན་དོན་དུ་ཤེས་ལྡན་དད་པས་སྐྱོབ༔

I constantly offer respectful homage.
Since the guide, the being Padmasambhava,
Has revealed these accounts of his successive births for the sake of those to be trained,
He protects the wise and faithful for the sake of the fortunate. ^2

> ཨེ་མ་ཧོ༔

> ཟ་འོག་དགུ་བརྩེགས་ཁྲི་ལ་སློབ་དཔོན་བཞུགས༔
> ཟ་འོག་ལྔ་བརྩེགས་ཁྲི་ལ་མཁན་པོ་བཞུགས༔
> གཡས་གྲལ་གཡོན་བྲལ་གཡོན་གྲལ་གབ་རྩེའི་གདན་སྟེང་ནས༔
> བེེ་རོས་གཙོ་བྱས་ལོ་ཙྭ་རྣམ་གསུམ་བཞུགས༔
> ནོར་སྣ་ཁྱད་འཕགས་མང་པོ་ཕུལ་ནས་སུ༔
> ཕྱག་སྟེ་མང་དུ་བཙལ་ནས་ཞུས་པ་ནི༔

Emaho!
Then, the Prince Mu-khri btsan-po,
Having invited the Master to the three-tiered central spire,
The Master sat upon a throne of nine-layered brocade,
The Abbot sat upon a throne of five-layered brocade,
And upon cushions of patterned silk in the right and left rows,
The three translators, headed by Vairocana, were seated.
After offering many extraordinary kinds of wealth,
He prostrated many times and made this request: ^3

> ཨེ་མ་ཧོ༔
> དུས་གསུམ་མཁྱེན་པའི་རྒྱལ་སྲས་ཆེན་པོ་ལགས༔
> ཡབ་རྗེ་ཁྲི་སྲོང་ལྡེའུ་བཙན་དགུང་དུ་གཤེགས༔
> བསྟན་པའི་སྒྲོན་མེ་སྲིན་པོའི་ཡུལ་དུ་གཤེགས༔
> བོད་ཀྱི་མི་རྣམས་ཚེ་ཐུང་གཡེང་བས་ཁེངས༔
> འདི་ནས་བཟུང་སྟེ་བསྐལ་བ་ཐ་མའི་བར༔
> སྐལ་ལྡན་དོན་དུ་དམ་ཆོས་དོན་འདུས་པ༔
> གུ་རུ་ཉིད་ལ་གསོལ་བ་གསོལ་བ་འདེབས་ནུས་པའི༔
> ཁྱེད་ཀྱིས་རྣམ་ཐར་མདོར་བསྡུས་གསུང་བར་ཞུ༔

Emaho!
O Great Bodhisattva who knows the three times,
My lord father Khri srong lde'u btsan has passed into the celestial realms,
And you, the lamp of the teachings, are departing for the land of the rakshasas.
The people of Tibet have short lives and are filled with distractions.
From now until the end of the eon,
For the sake of the fortunate, condensing the essence of the sacred Dharma,
And enabling one to pray to you, the Guru,
Please speak a concise account of your life and liberation. ^4

> དེ་སྐད་གསུང་པས་གུ་རུས་བཀའ་བསྩལ་པ༔
> ལེགས་པར་ཉོན་ཅིག་མུ་ཁྲི་བཙན་པོ་ཁྱོད༔
> མ་འོངས་དོན་དུ་བདག་གིས་མདོར་བསྡུས་བསྟན༔

When he had spoken thus, the Guru replied:
"Listen well, Mu-khri btsan-po!
For the sake of the future, I shall show a concise summary." ^5

> ཨེ་མ་ཧོ༔
> སྐྱེས་པའི་ཡབ་བརྒྱད་བལྟམས་པའི་ཡུམ་བརྒྱད་དང་༔
> སྤྲུལ་བའི་སྲས་བརྒྱད་བྱོན་པའི་ཡུལ་བརྒྱད་དང་༔
> བཞུགས་པའི་གནས་བརྒྱད་བསྟེན་པའི་བླ་མ་བརྒྱད༔
> ཞལ་གཟིགས་ཡི་དམ་མཐར་ཕྱིན་གྲུབ་རྟགས་བརྒྱད༔
> དུར་ཁྲོད་བརྒྱད་དང་ཆོས་བརྒྱུད་སློབ་མ་བརྒྱད༔
> དབེན་གནས་བརྒྱད་དང་ཐུགས་བྱོན་ཇོ་མོ་བརྒྱད༔
> མཛད་འཕྲིན་བརྒྱད་དང་སྦས་པའི་གཏེར་ཁ་བརྒྱད༔
> ཉམས་ལེན་ཟབ་ཆོས་བརྒྱད་དང་ཆོས་བདག་གླིང་པ་རྣམས་ཡོད༔

Emaho!
There are the eight fathers who engendered me and the eight mothers who gave me birth,
The eight lands where I appeared and the eight emanated sons,
The eight places where I resided and the eight gurus I attended,
The eight yidams whose faces I beheld and the eight signs of ultimate accomplishment,
The eight charnel grounds and the eight disciples of the Dharma lineage,
The eight solitary retreats and the eight ladies who arose in my heart,
The eight enlightened activities and the eight hidden treasure caches,
And the eight profound teachings of practice, along with the treasure-holding Lingpa masters. ^6

> དང་པོ་གུ་རུ་མཚན་བརྒྱད་བྱོན་ཚུལ་ནི༔
> ཁམས་གསུམ་སེམས་ཅན་བདེ་ལ་བཀོད༔

> བསྟན་པའི་མངའ་བདག་ཆོས་ཀྱི་རྗེ༔
> གུ་རུ་པདྨ་སཾ་བྷ་ཡིན༔
> སྐྱོན་གྱིས་མ་གོས་སྡེ་སྣོད་འཛིན༔
> གུ་རུ་པདྨ་རྒྱལ་པོ་ཡིན༔
> བདེ་བ་ཐམས་ཅད་སྐུ་ལ་རྫོགས༔
> གུ་རུ་རྡོ་རྗེ་གྲོ་ལོད་ཡིན༔
> ཐེག་དགུའི་མཉམ་ཉིད་ནམ་མཁར་བརྡལ༔
> གུ་རུ་ཉི་མ་འོད་ཟེར་ཡིན༔
> སྐུ་ལུས་གཅིག་ལ་བཀོད་པ་བརྒྱད༔
> གུ་རུ་ཤྰཀྱ་སེངྒེ་ཡིན༔
> རིགས་དྲུག་ཀུན་ལ་ཆོས་སྒྲ་སྒྲོག༔
> གུ་རུ་སེངྒེ་སྒྲ་སྒྲོག་ཡིན༔
> ཤེས་བྱ་ཐམས་ཅད་མཁྱེན་མཛད་པའི༔
> གུ་རུ་བློ་ལྡན་མཆོག་སྲེད་ཡིན༔
> གུ་རུ་མཚན་བརྒྱད་ཀྱི་ལེའུ་སྟེ་དང་པོའོ༔

Emaho!
First, as for how the eight names of the Guru appeared:
I establish the sentient beings of the three realms in bliss;
I am Guru Padma 'byung-gnas.
I am the sovereign of the teachings, the Lord of Dharma;
I am Guru Padma saṃbhava.
Untainted by flaws and embodying the three pitakas;
I am Guru Padma rgyal-po.
All bliss is perfected within my body;
I am Guru Rdo-rje gro-lod.
All vehicles are equalized, pervading like space;
I am Guru Nyi-ma 'od-zer.
Within a single body, the eight manifestations are displayed;
I am Guru Śākya seng-ge.
Proclaiming the sound of Dharma to all the six realms;
I am Guru Seng-ge sgra-sgrog.
Knowing all that can be known;
I am Guru Blo-ldan mchog-sred.
This is the first chapter, on the Eight Manifestations of the Guru. ^7

> གཉིས་པ་སྐྱེས་པའི་ཡབ་བརྒྱད་བསྟན་པ་ནི༔

> ལོངས་སྐུ་ཐུགས་རྗེ་ཆེན་པོ་ཡིན༔
> བསྟན་པའི་གཞི་མ་དགེ་འདུན་ཡབ༔

> རྒྱལ་པོ་ཨིནྡྲ་བོ་དྷི་ཡིན༔
> མཇལ་བ་སྔ་བ་མི་ཡི་ཡབ༔

> བརྩེ་བས་གྲོས་བཏབ་གྲོགས་ཀྱིས་ཡབ༔

Emaho!
Now, to show the eight fathers who begot me:
The father of the consciousness of basic space and awareness
Is the Dharmakāya Samantabhadra.
The father who has abandoned the two extremes of birth and death
Is the protector Amitāyus.
The father of all the noble ones of the six classes of beings
Is the Buddha Amitābha.
The father who is the root of the Dharma and bodhicitta
Is the Sambhogakāya, the Great Compassionate One.
The father who is the foundation of the teachings and the Saṅgha
Is the Nirmāṇakāya, Śākyamuni.
The father of the great clan and royalty
Is King Indrabodhi.
The human father whom I first met
Is the Dharma Minister Trinadzin.
The father-like friend who gave me loving counsel
Is the noble friend Ser-ö Dampa.
This is the second chapter, on the eight fathers who sired me. ^8

Emaho!
Ninth, the presentation of the eight signs of final accomplishment:
When I was exiled to the borders by the King of Oḍḍiyāna,
I displayed the sign of accomplishment by bringing the ḍākinīs under my control.
When the King of India impaled me on the point of a stake,
I displayed the sign of accomplishment by my limbs remaining unblemished.
When the heretic king cast me into the river,
I displayed the sign of accomplishment by not being carried away by the element of water.
When the King of Zahor attempted to burn me alive,
I displayed the sign of accomplishment by remaining unburned by the element of fire.
While serving as the chaplain to the King at Samye,
I displayed the sign of accomplishment by bringing the eight classes of spirits under my power.
When I stood proud before the Sovereign King,
Emanation fire scorched the King's robes;
I displayed the sign of accomplishment by proclaiming, "I am great! I am mighty!"
While the demonic ministers sowed discord and mocked,
I displayed the sign of accomplishment by their becoming lame, rigid, and dead.
When I acted as the subduer of the rākṣasas in the southwest,
I displayed the sign of accomplishment by establishing the rākṣasas in the Dharma.
This is the ninth chapter, on the display of the eight signs of accomplishment. ^15

Thirteenth, the presentation of the eight enlightened activities:
Unattached to samsara, I engaged in various playful displays;
Equal to all the Buddhas, I displayed various miraculous feats;
Through the conduct of yogic discipline, I performed the practice of the corpse;
Teaching the Dharma to beings, I acted as their mother and father;

Having turned the wheel of Dharma, I set foot in the land of Tibet;
Having caused the teachings to flourish, I concealed precious treasures;
Having suppressed the rākṣasas, I established the world in bliss.

Great King, listen well.
One such as I, Padmasambhava,
Possess the five common clairvoyances,
The five clairvoyances of the future,
The five clairvoyances of the present,
And the five immeasurable clairvoyances;
These five types of clairvoyance are known to me.
In former times, I was Amitābha, Boundless Light;
On Mount Potala, I was the Protector Avalokiteśvara;
At Dhanakośa, I was the form of Padmasambhava.
Although appearing as three merely as a mode of manifestation,
In reality, they are inseparable and not distinct from one another.
In the realm of the Dharmadhātu, I am Samantabhadra;
In the Dense Array, I am the Great Vajradhara;
At the Vajra Seat, I am the Great Sage himself;
Non-dual with them, I, Padma, am spontaneously present.
Therefore, pray to me constantly. ^23

> ང་ནི་མི་སྡོད་སྲིན་པོ་འདུལ་དུ་འགྲོ༔
> སྲིན་པོ་ཐམས་ཅད་ཆོས་ལ་བཀོད་ནས་འགྲོ༔
> བོད་འབངས་སེམས་ཅན་བདེ་ལ་བཀོད་ནས་འགྲོ། །

From the thirteen and eight generations of kings,
The Emperor Sechen will lead an army into Tibet,
Which will become the basis for the ruin of all Tibetan subjects;
Even my temples will be destroyed.
At that time, an emanation of mine named Sakya will appear,
Born in the upper region as the son of a father named Jampal
And a mother named Drolma.
Known as Drogon Phagpa Kunga Gyaltsen,
He will restore my temples,
And the teachings of the Secret Mantra will flourish and spread.
The Tibetan subjects and sentient beings will experience peace and happiness.
Thereafter, the kings of Tibet will engage in a scramble for power;
At times there will be Dharma kings, and at times demon kings.
The Dharma kings will establish the laws of religion,
While the demon kings will destroy those religious laws.
At that time, the people will experience all manner of prosperity and decline.
In a region of Dokham, as prophesied by the Buddha,
An emanation of Atiśa named Lobzang Drakpa will appear;
When that noble being comes, happiness will dawn for Tibet,
And at that time, the side of virtue will gain confidence.
A fortress will be established at Nyimado in Me-nyang,
And a temple will arise on the Red Hill in Lhasa.
My emanation, Orgyen Lingpa, will appear,
And at that time, he will reveal twenty-five treasure caches.
When he had spoken thus, the king shed tears.
Once again, prostrating and offering a mandala, he asked:
"O Great Master, guide of beings,
For how long will my royal lineage endure from this point on?
For how long will the happiness of the Tibetan subjects and sentient beings last?"
I request the Great Mahāguru to speak.
Thus he entreated him, prostrating until his forehead was worn away.
Then the Great Guru proclaimed:
"O King, from this point onward, your royal lineage
Will vanish in the upper regions like breath-mist fading from a mirror.
Thereafter, the kingship of Tibet will rotate;
In particular, an emanation of a bodhisattva
Bearing the name of the Bhadrakalpika Sūtra will appear.
This one with a mole on his face will hold the throne of Tibet,
And the sun of happiness will last only as long as a shadow across a skylight.
In the three years of the Horse, the Sheep, and the Monkey, the Chinese army will arrive;
In the Water-Male-Monkey year, a military camp will be established in Yarlung;
Ultimately, this Tibet will be brought under Chinese rule.
Internal strife among paternal cousins will break out on the heights of Tagogang;
From the direction of Bumthang, a corner of the throne will be suppressed.
An emanation of myself will appear to uphold the kingdom and the laws of the Dharma.
Conflict will arise in the fortresses of Nepal,
And the sun of happiness will briefly dawn for Tibet.
Having engaged in evil conduct yourself, you call it an evil age;
The times do not change; it is the people who change.
At that time, people will have no merit or reputation.
Constantly offer prayers to me, Padma;
When it is daytime, meditate on the Great Compassionate One;
For the sake of sentient beings, recite the Mani essence mantra.
Bearing in mind the kindness of your parents, the six classes of beings, I shall come.
When night falls, meditate on me, Padmasambhava;
For your own benefit, recite the Vajra Guru mantra.
I shall protect all sentient beings of the degenerate age;
I shall watch over them continuously throughout the three times.
Even if they are born in the three lower realms, I shall come to rescue them.
Although I have been very kind to Tibet, they do not recognize my kindness.
If you can pray to me continuously,
There is a way to avert the degenerate age of a ten-year lifespan.
An emanation of Maitreya called 'Seven Cubits' will appear,
And the sufferings of the evil eon of a ten-year lifespan will be liberated.
Once again, the eon will increase and become joyful;
Visions of the Buddha will occur again and again.
Every morning and every evening, I shall come for the sake of Tibet;
For those with faith, Padmasambhava sleeps at their door.
For my body, there is neither death nor birth.
You, known as the Prince Mutri Tsenpo,
Shall work for the benefit of beings for seventeen lifetimes from now,
And then depart for the land of the dakinis in Uddiyana.
You, the great translator Vairocana,
Hide this profound holy Dharma of mine, Padma,
As a treasure in the central spire of glorious Samye;
Entrust it to those with the karmic destiny in the future, he said.
I will not remain, but go to subdue the rakshasas;
I go to lead all the rakshasas to the Dharma;
Having established the people of Tibet in happiness, I shall now depart." ^24
